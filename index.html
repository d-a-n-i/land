How this works

Your phone's GPS gives position and ground speed (works in airplane mode - GPS is receive-only; a window seat helps). Distance is great-circle to the destination runway area. Time left = cruise at your real ground speed (wind included), then a descent profile from ~200 km out, then a final approach leg plus typical airport vectoring time. When online, the route comes from adsbdb and live ADS-B from adsb.lol is tried; everything needed is cached so it keeps working offline.
