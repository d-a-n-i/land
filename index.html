How this works

Nothing to type: it starts GPS on open and assumes Tel Aviv (TLV) unless your track clearly points elsewhere, then it guesses the airport ahead (tap the destination to fix it; a flight number works there too). Your phone's GPS gives position and ground speed (works in airplane mode - GPS is receive-only; a window seat helps). Distance is great-circle to the destination runway area. Time left = cruise at your real ground speed (wind included), then a descent profile from ~200 km out, then a final approach leg plus typical airport vectoring time. When online, the route comes from adsbdb and live ADS-B from adsb.lol is tried; everything needed is cached so it keeps working offline.
